SVY1110 – Introduction to Global Navigation Satellite Systems

GNSS data collection

Project overview

In this student assignment, I collected GNSS observations using a phone in different surroundings, including open sky, areas near trees and areas near buildings. The task involved exporting recorded observations for analysis.

Learning and relevance

Different environments can affect satellite visibility and positioning data. The assignment provided an opportunity to practise collecting and organising technical information.
