ENP3103 – Engineering Methodology Research

Rock joint research proposal

Project overview

I developed a proposed research project about shear load transfer in rock joints filled with clayey soil. The proposed method considers different infill thicknesses and moisture contents, with direct shear testing as the planned approach.

Learning and relevance

This is a research proposal and presentation, not a claim that laboratory tests were completed. The work included planning research questions, a testing method, safety considerations and a presentation.
